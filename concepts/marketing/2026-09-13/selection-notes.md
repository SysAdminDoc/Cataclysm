# Marketing visual selection

## Identity

The existing wavefront and fault mark stays. It is clear at small sizes, matches the product palette, and has a direct connection to the simulator. Replacing it would discard a stronger identity for novelty.

Source and selected copies are stored in this folder. The release gate should verify that the selected logo remains byte-for-byte identical to `assets/branding/logo.svg`.

## Hero

Selected: `candidates/hero-candidate-a.png`

Published asset: `assets/marketing/cataclysm-hero.png`

Why it won:

- The promise is readable before the embedded interface.
- The product image is real and large enough to prove the app exists.
- The dark editorial layout matches Cataclysm without inventing a new visual system.
- The crop removes the app header and every release number.
- It still reads cleanly when reduced to a GitHub social-card size.

Candidate B remains in the archive as the rejected product-stage direction. It gives the screenshot more space, but its headline and proof compete for attention.

## README gallery

Lead with the dark Tohoku outcome. Follow with asteroid and nuclear results as a two-column comparison, then use source science as the credibility proof. Keep run history, settings, and the light theme in the archive and supporting documentation rather than the main pitch.
